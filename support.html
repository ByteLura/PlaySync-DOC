Frequently asked questions

Does PlaySync Pro work in standalone builds?

The tools run exclusively in the Unity Editor. The [AutoSavePlayMode] attribute and PlaySyncPreset containers compile cleanly into runtime builds with zero overhead.

Why didn't the Quick Restore Window pop up when I exited Play Mode?

The popup only triggers if active serialized changes were detected during playback. You can also verify that "Auto-Open Restore Window" is checked in Auto-Save Settings.

Where are Flight Recorder history files stored?

In [Project]/Library/PlaySyncHistory/. They stay local to your machine and do not pollute your Git repository.
